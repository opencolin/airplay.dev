Put anything on your Samsung TVs, from Claude.

Paste one prompt. Claude sets up a small hub on your Mac, finds the Samsung TVs and monitors on your network, and from then on you just ask: “put our dashboard on both 98-inch screens.”

1Copy this prompt and paste it into Claude Code (terminal, desktop app or IDE) on a Mac that’s on the same network as the screens.


  

    
    View SKILL.md on GitHub
  

  
2Claude installs the hub and scans for screens. Approve the steps it asks about.

  
3Ask for what you want on the screens.





Things to ask


    
  
  • “Show example.com on every monitor.”
    • 
  
  • “Put this image on the conference room TV.” (attach or point to a file)
    • 
  
  • “Clear the 98-inch screens.”
    • 
  
  • “Switch the monitors back to PC.”
    • 
  
  • “Which screens are asleep?”
    • 




Good to know


    
  
  • The hub runs on your Mac and needs Node, ffmpeg and Google Chrome. Claude checks for them and asks before installing anything.
    • 
  
  • It works with Samsung smart TVs and Smart Monitors that accept DLNA casting, on the same network as your Mac.
    • 
  
  • The hub starts at login and keeps screens showing what you asked for, even after they sleep.
    • 
  
  • Prefer clicking? Once it’s running, the dashboard is at http://localhost:8787.
    • 
  
  • The code is open: github.com/opencolin/samsung-wall.
